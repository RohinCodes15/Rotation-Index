How input freshness is checked

The model reads each game's normalized possession file, built from the raw play-by-play. The audit records a fingerprint of both files for every game it verifies. An input is fresh when today's files still match those fingerprints exactly. Any change after the audit, even reformatting, makes it stale until the audit is rerun.

Limitations

  • The data is full-game totals for each five-man lineup, not single possessions. Which opposing five was on the floor is not available, so opponents are adjusted at the team level only.
  • Ridge regression shrinks everyone toward 0. Players with few possessions are pulled hardest, so their estimates say little.
  • This is one season of plus-minus. It is noisy and cannot separate offense from defense yet.
  • A difference of 1–2 points between two players is usually within the noise.
